# firedough

The landing page of **Firedough Pizza**, a wood-fired pizza truck near Strandhill, Co. Sligo,
on the Wild Atlantic Way of Ireland. The client is Firedough Pizza. One page: hero, about,
menu, community, footer. Next.js 14 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn-style
primitives, on Vercel. The rules of this repo are in `.icm/project-rules.md`.
